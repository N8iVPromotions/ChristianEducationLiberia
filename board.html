On the Ground in Liberia

Harry Gorwor — School Headmaster

While the U.S. board handles governance and fundraising, Harry Gorwor is the heart and hands of CEL on the ground in Kakata, Liberia.

Harry founded Gifts of Hope Community School in 2018, growing it from 9 teachers and 136 students into a thriving institution that continues to expand. He manages day-to-day operations, oversees 35 staff members, and ensures every dollar sent from the U.S. is used faithfully.

"These children are not forgotten by God. They should not be forgotten by us." — Harry Gorwor

How We Operate

Governance & Accountability

CEL is committed to the highest standards of non-profit governance, transparency, and donor accountability.

🏛️

501(c)(3) Status

CEL is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization with the IRS. All donations are tax-deductible under U.S. federal law. EIN available upon request.

📋

Annual Reporting

The board files annual IRS Form 990 returns and maintains complete financial records. We welcome donor inquiries about our financials at any time.

🌍

Liberian Registration

Gifts of Hope Community School is officially registered with the Liberian government, ensuring legal standing, accountability, and recognition for students.
