Our Mission

Providing Christ-centered education to the children of Liberia and offering hope for their future.

Our Mission Statement

CEL exists to provide a Christ-centered education to the children of Liberia and offer hope for their future.

"Every lesson taught, every child who learns to read, every graduate who goes on to serve — it is a testament to God's transforming power at work in Liberia."

Through Gifts of Hope Community School in Kakata, Liberia, CEL is reaching children who would otherwise have no access to education — offering not just academic instruction but holistic care, community, and the love of Christ.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Four pillars guide everything we do at Gifts of Hope Community School.

✝️

Christ-Centered

Faith is not an add-on — it is woven into every lesson, relationship, and moment of school life. We believe education rooted in Christ transforms not just the mind but the whole person.

📚

Quality Education

We hold our students to rigorous academic standards from Preschool through Grade 5. Every child deserves excellent teaching, proper materials, and the opportunity to achieve their full potential.

🤝

Community Impact

Gifts of Hope serves rural children in Kakata who have no other access to schooling. By educating one child, we uplift an entire family — and by extension, the broader community.

❤️

Whole-Child Care

Education does not happen in a vacuum. We provide medical care, emotional support, and a safe environment so that every student can come to school ready to learn and to thrive.

Looking Ahead

Our Vision for Liberia

We envision a Liberia where every child — regardless of location or family income — has access to a quality, Christ-centered education.

We see a generation of Liberians rising to lead their communities, their churches, and their nation — equipped with knowledge, character, and faith. Gifts of Hope Community School is one school, but every school like it is a seed planted in hope for a transformed nation.

Liberia is rebuilding. After decades of conflict, the country is moving forward — and education is the engine of that renewal. CEL believes that Christ-centered schools are among the most powerful investments a community can make in its future.

How We Operate

A Lean, High-Impact Model

Gifts of Hope Community School is located in Kakata, Margibi County, Liberia — a rural area where access to schooling is extremely limited. The school is led on the ground by Harry Gorwor, founder, pastor, and headmaster, supported by a dedicated team of 35 staff members.

In the United States, Christian Education in Liberia (CEL) operates as a 501(c)(3) non-profit. U.S. board members volunteer their time entirely, ensuring that 100% of every donation flows directly to the school in Liberia — not to administration, overhead, or salaries in the U.S.

The school is also registered with the Liberian government, ensuring full accountability on both sides of the Atlantic. This dual-registration model is a hallmark of CEL's commitment to transparency and stewardship.
