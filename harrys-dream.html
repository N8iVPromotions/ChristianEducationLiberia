Founder's Story

From a Dream to a School

Harry Gorwor has spent his life as an educator and pastor in Liberia. For decades he worked in classrooms, churches, and communities — watching children in rural areas around Kakata grow up without ever setting foot in a school. He carried a dream: to reach those children.

In July 2016, Harry was given access to two acres of land in the bush near Kakata. To most, it was overgrown land at the edge of a community. To Harry, it was the beginning of something extraordinary.

What followed was months — then years — of hard work, prayer, fundraising, and construction. Harry rallied supporters, cleared land, built classrooms, and recruited teachers who shared his vision. Every obstacle was met with faith.

"These children are not forgotten by God. They should not be forgotten by us."

On September 8, 2018, that dream became a reality. Gifts of Hope Community School opened its doors to 136 children ages 5–12 — children who had never sat in a classroom, many of whom had never held a pencil. With 9 dedicated teachers and a school built by faith, Harry's dream was alive.

Still Growing

Harry Still Leads

Harry Gorwor remains the headmaster and heart of Gifts of Hope Community School. From the very first day classes were held to the present, he has been there — teaching, pastoring, leading, and pouring his life into his students.

What was once a dream sketched on a heart is now a school with 35 staff members, a full curriculum, medical care for every student, and a community that has changed because children are being educated and loved.

100% of every donation received by CEL flows directly to the school — covering salaries, supplies, medical costs, and registration fees. Our U.S. board volunteers their time so that nothing is withheld from the children.
