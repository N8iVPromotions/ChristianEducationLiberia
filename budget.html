Full Breakdown

Monthly Operating Budget: $7,500

Every single dollar donated to CEL goes directly to Gifts of Hope Community School. Our U.S.-based board volunteers their time — no salaries, no administrative fees taken from your gift.

The $7,500 monthly budget covers everything required to keep 35 staff employed and 136+ children learning, healthy, and cared for.

Staff Salaries — 65% ($4,875/mo)

Pays the monthly salaries of 35 dedicated teachers, administrators, and support staff. In Liberia, average teacher salaries are just $150/month — CEL ensures every staff member is fairly compensated.

School Supplies — 15% ($1,125/mo)

Textbooks, notebooks, pencils, chalk, and teaching materials for all 136+ students across Preschool through Grade 5.

Student Medical Care — 12% ($900/mo)

Basic medical care, health screenings, and medicine for students. Many children arrive at school with untreated illnesses — this care is life-changing.

Government Registration — 8% ($600/mo)

Annual fees for official registration with the Liberian government, ensuring the school's legal standing and credibility.

Our Commitment

Zero Overhead — 100% to the School

We are proud that every single cent donated goes directly to Gifts of Hope Community School. Here is how we do it.

🤝

Volunteer U.S. Board

All U.S.-based board members and administrators donate their time entirely. No salaries, no stipends. Their commitment is a gift to the mission.

📬

Direct Funds Transfer

Donations are transferred directly to the school in Liberia. No middlemen, no layers. Harry Gorwor receives funds and manages on-the-ground operations.

📊

Full Accountability

CEL maintains records of all expenditures. The school is registered with the Liberian government and operates with full accountability to donors and authorities.
