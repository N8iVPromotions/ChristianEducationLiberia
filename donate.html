Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

  • 100% of every donation goes directly to Gifts of Hope Community School in Kakata, Liberia. Funds cover teacher and staff salaries (approximately 65% of the monthly budget), school supplies (15%), student medical care (12%), and Liberian government registration fees (8%). Our U.S. board members volunteer their time so that nothing is taken for administrative overhead.

  • Yes. Christian Education in Liberia (CEL) is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization recognized by the IRS. All donations made to CEL are fully tax-deductible to the extent permitted by U.S. law. You will receive a donation receipt via celonline.org for your records.

  • Gifts of Hope Community School is funded entirely through the generous donations of supporters like you. The school requires approximately $7,500 per month to operate — covering 35 staff members and providing education, supplies, and medical care for 136+ children. There is no government subsidy or tuition charged to families. Your generosity is literally what keeps the school open.

  • Yes! Recurring monthly gifts are warmly welcomed and provide the school with consistent, reliable support. Monthly giving is one of the most powerful ways to make a lasting impact — even $55/month covers a child's full education and care for the year. You can set up a recurring gift at celonline.org/donate.
