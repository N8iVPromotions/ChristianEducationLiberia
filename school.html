About the School

A School Born from a Dream and Built on Faith

Located outside Kakata in Margibi County, Liberia, Gifts of Hope Community School was founded to reach children in the surrounding rural area who had no access to schooling of any kind.

The school officially opened on September 8, 2018, welcoming its first class of Preschool through Grade 3 students. Many of these children — ages 5 to 12 — had never held a pencil. Some had never heard a complete sentence read aloud.

Today those same children are reading, writing, solving math problems, and worshipping God together. Gifts of Hope has grown to serve students through Grade 5, with a full team of 35 dedicated Liberian educators and support staff pouring their hearts into every child, every day.

Our Journey

School Milestones

From a dream in a pastor's heart to a thriving school community — this is the story of Gifts of Hope.

September 8, 2018

Grand Opening

Gifts of Hope Community School welcomed its very first students — 136 children and 9 teachers — to a school many in the community had prayed years to see. Many children attended their first-ever day of school.

Government Registration

Officially Recognized

Gifts of Hope Community School achieved full registration with the Liberian government — a critical milestone ensuring accountability, legitimacy, and the school's long-term standing in the community.

Staff Expansion

Grown to 35 Staff Members

As enrollment grew and grade levels expanded, the school's team grew with it. Today 35 dedicated staff members — teachers, administrators, and support personnel — serve students daily.

Medical Care Program

Health & Wellness for Every Student

Recognizing that children cannot learn when they are sick, CEL added a dedicated medical care program providing health services to all enrolled students — addressing a critical gap in rural Liberia's healthcare access.

What We Offer

Programs at Gifts of Hope

Our programs address the whole child — mind, body, and spirit — because every dimension of a child's life matters to God.

📚

Academic Education

A rigorous, structured curriculum from Preschool through Grade 5 covering reading, writing, mathematics, social studies, and science. Many students arrive having never held a pencil — and leave able to read and solve problems with confidence.

✝️

Christ-Centered Faith Formation

Faith is woven into every class, every morning devotion, and every relationship at Gifts of Hope. Students learn not only academic skills but the love of Christ, the Word of God, and what it means to live as people of faith and character.

🩺

Student Health & Medical Care

All enrolled students receive access to medical care through the school's health program — because a sick child cannot learn, and no child should miss school due to a preventable or treatable health condition.
