Founder's Story

Harry's Dream

Harry Gorwor has spent his life as an educator and pastor in Liberia. For years he carried a dream — to reach children in rural communities outside Kakata who had no access to schooling, no chance to read, and no opportunity to experience Christ's love through education.

"These children are not forgotten by God. They should not be forgotten by us."

On September 8, 2018, that dream became reality when Gifts of Hope Community School opened its doors to 136 children ages 5–12 who had never sat in a classroom. Today Harry serves as Headmaster, and what began as a dream is now a thriving school community.
