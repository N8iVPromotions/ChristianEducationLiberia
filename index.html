Founder's Story

Harry's Dream

Harry Gorwor has spent his life as an educator and pastor in Liberia. For years, he carried a vision — a dream to reach the children in rural communities outside of Kakata who had no access to schooling, no chance to learn to read, and no opportunity to experience Christ's love through education.

"These children are not forgotten by God. They should not be forgotten by us."

On September 8, 2018, that dream became reality when Gifts of Hope Community School opened its doors to 136 children ages 5–12 — children who had never sat in a classroom. With 9 dedicated teachers and a heart full of faith, Harry welcomed them all.

Today, Harry serves as Headmaster (Principal) of the school. What began as a dream is now a growing community of learners, educators, and believers — all supported by Christians around the world through CEL.

Gifts of Hope Community School

Where Hope
Takes Root

Located outside Kakata in Margibi County, Liberia, Gifts of Hope Community School was founded to reach children in the surrounding rural area who would otherwise never have a chance to attend school, learn to read and write, or experience Christ's love firsthand.

The school opened with Preschool through Grade 3, serving children ages 5–12. Many of these students had never held a pencil before their first day. Today they are reading, writing, solving math problems, and singing praises to God.
