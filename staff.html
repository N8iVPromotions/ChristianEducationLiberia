The People Behind the Mission

Liberian Educators Transforming Their Own Communities

The heart of Gifts of Hope Community School beats in the lives of 35 dedicated Liberian educators and support staff who show up every day to invest in the next generation.

These are men and women who believe deeply in the mission of Christian Education in Liberia — not just as a job, but as a calling. They know firsthand the pain of a nation torn apart by war, and they have chosen to be part of the answer.

The average teacher salary in Liberia is just $150 per month — a sobering reminder of the economic realities these educators navigate. CEL is committed to paying fair, reliable wages funded entirely by the generosity of our donors. When you give to CEL, you are putting money directly into the hands of people transforming their communities one child at a time.

Behind the Scenes

Support Staff

A school is more than its classrooms. These dedicated team members ensure every child is healthy, safe, and supported every single day.

🩺

Medical & Health Staff

Provides basic healthcare, health screenings, and wellness check-ins for all 136+ students. In rural Liberia, access to medical care is a privilege — Gifts of Hope makes it a right for every enrolled child. Sick children are seen promptly so they never have to miss class due to an untreated condition.

📋

Administrative Support

Handles enrollment records, government compliance documentation, donor reporting, and family communications. This team keeps the school legally registered, financially accountable, and running smoothly — allowing teachers to focus entirely on their students.

🔧

Facilities & Maintenance

Maintains the school building, grounds, and learning environment so that every classroom is clean, safe, and welcoming. In a region where infrastructure is fragile, the facilities team's work is the invisible foundation that makes everything else possible.
